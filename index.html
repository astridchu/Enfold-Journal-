Journal

There is a world within.

A quiet place to return to after each session. See what moved, what grew, and what kind of journey it was.

Each session leaves a trace.

Your reflections and your body’s signals become a gentle report. Not a score. A memory you can come back to.

Watch your inner world grow.

Your Alignment Tree grows from what you discover. Twelve archetypes give each session a name.
